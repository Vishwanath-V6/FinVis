# FinVis Presentation Outline

1. Problem: annual reports hide figures across hundreds of pages and layouts.
2. Live workflow: upload PDF, inspect status, open dashboard.
3. Architecture: staged cache, selective OCR, table row chunks, FAISS.
4. Data integrity: units, periods, scope, original evidence.
5. Financial analysis: trends, period changes, margins.
6. Grounded Q&A: direct table resolution, citations, abstention.
7. Evaluation: Hit@K, MRR, answer/citation/abstention metrics.
8. Limitations: complex layouts, OCR quality, and pretrained extractive QA.
9. Local deployment and next steps.
